Welcome to the Street

Dope Wars - King of the Street is the ultimate street empire simulator. You start with a small wad of cash and a dream. Buy product, avoid the heat, and flip your way to the top. But watch out — rival dealers want your turf, and the cops are always watching. Do you have what it takes to become the King of the Street?

How to Play

Master the street economy with these simple rules:

  • Buy Low: Check the market prices and buy when product is cheap.
  • Sell High: Prices fluctuate daily. Sell when you see a profit.
  • Manage Inventory: Don't hold too much or the heat will catch up.
  • Avoid the Cops: Random police raids can wipe your inventory. Stay alert.
  • Build Your Rep: Higher reputation unlocks better deals and locations.
  • Goal: Turn $1,000 into $1,000,000 to become the King.

💡 Pro Tip: Buy when prices are in the green, sell in the red. Always keep some cash in reserve.
